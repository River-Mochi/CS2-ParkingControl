# Parking Control

**No Street Parking — citywide, by district, by road size, or individual roads — without changing your road design.**

Parking Control prevents cars and motorcycles from choosing eligible roadside parking in Cities: Skylines II.

Choose where you want parking removed while keeping normal parking lots, garages, and building parking available.

## Features

### Choose where

- **by District** — recommended/default. Enable **Roadside Parking Ban** in the district's normal Policies panel.
- **Manual Only** — no city or district ban. Use the **No Parking** road button wherever you want.
- **Whole City** — blocks eligible street parking citywide, except roads with painted angled or perpendicular parking spaces.

The manual **No Parking** road tool works with any of the three modes above.

### 4-lane and 6-lane roads

Optional citywide settings can also ban roadside parking on:

- **4-lane roads**
- **6-lane roads**

These settings work in addition to the main dropdown and manual road bans.

They also work with custom **Road Builder (RB)** roads when they have the matching number of driving lanes.

Roads with built-in painted angled or perpendicular parking spaces are left alone by the automatic Whole City and 4-/6-lane bans.

### Manual No Parking road tool

Use the **No Parking** button in the Roads Services tools to control individual road segments.

- **Left-click** to add No Parking.
- **Right-click** to remove No Parking.
- Works on small roads and 1-way alleys too.
- Manual No Parking can still remove parking from roads with painted angled or perpendicular spaces if you specifically choose that road.

### Parking status

Optional status rows show:

- Street-parking enforcement
- Manual No Parking roads
- Public, street, and building parking use
- Parking availability
- Personal vehicle locations

An on-demand diagnostic report is also available from the **About** tab.

## Good to know

- Existing parked cars gradually relocate after parking is banned. Large citywide changes can take some time to clear.
- Parking lots, garages, and normal building parking remain available.
- Make sure your city has enough off-street parking. When parking is unavailable, the game may use outside-connection vehicle storage/staging as it does in vanilla.
- Some roads already do not allow street parking, such as highways, small 2-way alleys, and some 3-lane asymmetric roads.
- New or rebuilt roads may briefly accept a few cars while CS2 updates their parking lanes.
- No need to add grass, trees, or wide sidewalks just to remove roadside parking. Keep the road style you want.
- Bicycles are untouched.
- Removing the mod returns streets to normal game behavior after the city is reloaded.

## Road splitting

Manual **No Parking** is stored per road segment.

If another road or track crosses a manually banned road and CS2 splits that road into two new segments, re-apply **No Parking** to the new halves.

District, Whole City, and 4-/6-lane automatic bans are not affected by road splitting.

## Compatibility

Optional integration with **Mochi's Hover Colors** lets the manual No Parking tool follow its Bulldozer + Roads hover colors, like the normal road tools.

Parking Control uses the game's own parking-lane behavior and does not narrow roads, widen sidewalks, or add driving lanes.
