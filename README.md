# Parking Control

**No Street Parking — citywide or by district, without changing your roads.**

Parking Control prevents cars and motorcycles from choosing eligible roadside parking in Cities: Skylines II. Use it across the whole city or only in the districts you choose.

## Features

**Dropdown choices**
- **Whole City** — blocks eligible street parking citywide and is the default.
- **by District** — enable **Roadside Parking Ban** in the district's normal Policies panel.
- **OFF** — restores normal street parking.

- Parking lots and building parking remain available.
- Optional status rows show street use, parking-space use, and car locations.
- An on-demand diagnostic report is available from the About tab.

## Good to know

Existing parked vehicles are not deleted. They leave naturally when their owners next use them, which can take time.

- New or rebuilt roads may briefly accept a few cars while CS2 updates their parking lanes.
- No need to add grass, trees, or wide sidewalks just to remove roadside parking. Keep the road style you want and decide where street parking belongs.
- Make sure your city has enough off-street parking. Parking availability affects vehicle pathfinding and travel choices.
- Bicycles are untouched. Removing the mod returns streets to normal game behavior after the city is reloaded.
